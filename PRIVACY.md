# LazyChamp Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 30, 2026**

## Overview

LazyChamp is a browser extension for Pokémon Showdown that helps users convert Pokémon sets and teams between standard EV distributions and Pokémon Champions Points.

LazyChamp is designed to process team information locally in the browser. It does not collect, sell, or transmit users' personal information or Pokémon team contents to the developer or to external analytics or advertising services.

## Information Accessed and Processed

### Pokémon Team Content

When users use LazyChamp's conversion features, the extension accesses Pokémon sets and team information in the Pokémon Showdown page.

This information is processed locally in the browser to perform stat conversions, Speed Creep calculations, and team modifications.

LazyChamp does not transmit Pokémon team contents to the developer or external services.

Team modifications occur only after an explicit user action. An Undo function is available until the page is reloaded.

### Locally Stored Preferences

LazyChamp stores two preferences locally in the browser:

- Preferred target format.
- Speed Creep Assumption setting.

These preferences are used to remember the user's settings. They are not transmitted to the developer or external services.

Users can remove these preferences by clearing the relevant Pokémon Showdown website data in their browser.

### Public Pokédex Data

LazyChamp may request Pokémon Showdown's publicly available Pokédex data from the same website where the extension operates.

This data is used for Pokémon information and calculations. A bundled backup is available if the request fails.

The request does not include users' Pokémon team contents.

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

LazyChamp does not:

- Sell or rent user data.
- Share Pokémon team contents with third parties.
- Send personal information to the developer.
- Use analytics or tracking services.
- Display advertisements or collect advertising data.

The browser communicates with Pokémon Showdown when retrieving its public Pokédex data. That request is subject to the website's own handling of ordinary web requests.

## Data Security and User Control

Because LazyChamp processes team information locally, the developer does not maintain a database of users' teams or personal information.

Users remain in control of when team conversions occur. They can also clear locally stored preferences through their browser's website-data settings.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if LazyChamp's functionality or data-handling practices change. Any updated version will be published in this repository.
